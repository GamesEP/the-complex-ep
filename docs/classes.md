# Classes

How the three test classes are built: choosing one, what it changes, and the six abilities that
came with them. The design is [plans/gdd/systems.md](../plans/gdd/systems.md) ("Clases"); it is a
deliberate, temporary exception to pillar 1 so the gameplay can be tried with distinct roles.

| | |
|---|---|
| `shared/core/Config/Classes` | The classes (stats, weapons, kit, passive) and all nine abilities (name, cooldown, ready attribute, colour). |
| `shared/features/classes/Classes` | Pure: which ability is in which slot, ownership, damage multipliers, "behind", execute. |
| `server/features/classes/ClassService` | The `ChooseClass` remote, the `Class` attribute, and `isPaused`. |
| `server/features/combat/CombatService` | Applies a class (health, speed, weapons), the passives, and the six new abilities. |
| `server/features/enemies/EnemyService` | The pause, taunt targeting, and the shockwave's push and slow. |
| `client/features/classes/ui/ClassSelect` | The selection screen. |
| `client/features/combat/CombatInput`, `ui/CombatHud`, `Effects` | Firing, drawing and showing whatever the class has in each slot. |

## The class is an attribute

`Class` on the Player: `""` until chosen, then a key of `Config.CLASSES`. The selection screen opens
and closes on it, the HUD and the ability key read it, and it survives respawns because it is on
the Player, not the character. `ClassService.choose` accepts a choice **once per session**: a
second one would hand a player in the middle of a fight full health and fresh cooldowns. Whether
the class may be changed mid-room is still open in the GDD.

Applying a class is `CombatService.applyClass`, passed to ClassService as a dependency: health and
max health, base walk speed (fed into `Defense.walkSpeed` like the old constant), cooldowns reset,
and weapons re-equipped. `onCharacter` reads the same attribute, so a respawn keeps the class.

## The pause

`ClassService.isPaused()` is true while any player in the server has no class. `EnemyService.step`
returns at once while it is: no movement, no attacks, no projectiles, no grab drain. The waves still
spawn (static) — the director only counts. With more than one player, one person on the selection
screen freezes the board for everybody; that is fine for testing and is the thing to revisit when
the lobby replaces the screen.

## Weapons

Each class names a `weapon` and an optional `offHandWeapon` (`Config.CLASSES`). They are `.rbxmx`
models generated by `scripts/generate-models.luau` (`WeaponGreatsword`, `WeaponShield`,
`WeaponDagger`, `WeaponStaff`) under the same convention as the test sword: `Grip` at the origin,
the weapon along -Z. Each model carries `GripPitch` / `GripYaw` / `GripRoll` attributes (degrees),
and `CombatService.equipWeapon` welds `Grip` to the hand's grip attachment turned by them — so how a
weapon is held lives with the weapon, not in a table in code. The staff is pitched 165° so it stands
upright with the orb above the head (measured: orb 3.2 studs above the hand).

The combat poses (`CombatAnim`) and in particular the block guard were solved for the test sword
at 70°. The daggers and greatsword share that pitch; the shield and the staff do not, and nobody has
looked at them in every pose yet.

## Damage

Every player hit on an enemy goes through `CombatService.hitEnemy`: the base (with the damage
buff) times `Classes.outgoingMultiplier`, which is the class's `damageMultiplier` and, for the
Damage class, `+DPS_PASSIVE_BONUS` against a *vulnerable* enemy — one in its `recover` phase (the
window after its own attack, and the parry stun) or hit from behind (`Classes.isBehind`).

Every hit on a player goes through `CombatService.hurt`, which now first multiplies by
`Classes.incomingMultiplier` (the Tank's passive) and by `1 - PROTECT_FIELD_REDUCTION` when the
player stands in a Support field. `hurt` takes an optional `origin`; the shots and the rail-beam
pass theirs, and a hit whose origin is inside the Tank's raised Muralla (`BulwarkUntil`,
`BULWARK_ARC`) is reported as a landed `"block"` with no damage, so a projectile is stopped by the
shield instead of flying on. The Muralla is checked after the dash's invulnerability and before a
parry.

## Abilities

The three original abilities keep their own remotes (`Spin`, `Stinger`, `OverDrive`) and their
attribute names; each now also refuses a player whose class does not own it. The six new ones share
one contracted remote, `UseAbility(id, aim)`, dispatched by `CombatService.useAbility` through
`ABILITY_HANDLERS` after the same checks (owned, not grabbed/blocking/stunned/charging, off
cooldown). Every cooldown is `Config.ABILITIES[id].readyAttribute` in server time, which is how the
HUD draws any slot without knowing which ability is in it.

| Ability | Class | What the server does |
|---|---|---|
| Provocación (`taunt`) | Tank | `EnemyService.taunt` on every enemy in `TAUNT_RADIUS`; target assignment then forces the tank as their target until `tauntUntil`. |
| Muralla (`bulwark`) | Tank | Sets `BulwarkUntil`; see Damage above. |
| Ejecución (`execute`) | Damage | Frontal arc; `Classes.executeDamage` triples it at or below `EXECUTE_THRESHOLD` of max health. |
| Pulso curativo (`healPulse`) | Support | Heals every living player in the radius, caster included. |
| Campo protector (`protectField`) | Support | A field in `protectFields`; see Damage above. Expired by the support tick. |
| Onda de choque (`shockwave`) | Support | Hits, then `EnemyService.shove`: a push velocity for `SHOCKWAVE_PUSH_SECONDS`, then the enemy's whole step runs at `SHOCKWAVE_SLOW_FACTOR` of real time. |

The Support's passive and the protect fields' expiry run on one 1-second tick
(`CombatService.startSupportTick`).

Visuals for the new abilities come from a `CombatVfx` broadcast to every client, the caster
included, so nobody draws them twice; the caster's own pose (`Effects.classAbilityLocal`) is the
only local prediction. They are simple placeholders (rings, a dome, a shield plate, a slash), and
only the Execution has an animation (the combo finisher).

## Measured in Studio

Selection screen open with three cards and the board frozen (0 of 3 enemies moved in 2 s); a real
mouse click on "Elegir" gave `Class = tank`, 160 max health, greatsword and shield, the screen
closed and enemies moving. Damage class: 85 health, a dagger in each hand, HUD showing Stinger /
Ejecución / Over Drive; Execution killed a full-health Centinela. Support: 100 health, staff
upright; the shockwave pushed an enemy 10.6 studs (configured 10). Taunt and Muralla fired from the
keyboard and started their cooldowns; a Tank firing `Stinger` or `healPulse` by remote was refused.

## Not verified

That taunted enemies actually switch target, that the Muralla stops a shot, that the protect field
cuts damage, and the exact heal amounts — Roblox's built-in character regeneration (1% of max
health per second) is added on top and swamps the numbers in a test. The new weapons have not been
checked in every pose, and the selection screen has not been seen on a phone.
