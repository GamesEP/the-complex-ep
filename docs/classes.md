# Abilities & Legacy Class Definitions

> **VS-01 update:** Fixed starter classes (`Tanque`, `Daño`, `Apoyo`) were retired to uphold Pillar
> 1 (*everyone starts equal with the base sword and builds their kit from 1-of-3 run upgrades*).
> `Config/Classes.luau` still holds the shared catalog of all **9 active abilities** (`Torbellino`,
> `Rugido`, `Coraza`, `Estocada`, `Vórtice`, `Corte Final`, `Santuario`, `Barrera`, `Repulsión`)
> that players draft and level up via `UpgradeService` (`docs/upgrades.md`). The start screen
> (`ClassSelect.luau`) now acts as the **Difficulty & Start Run** screen (`ChooseDifficulty` +
> `StartRun`).

| | |
|---|---|
| `shared/core/Config/Classes` | The classes (stats, weapons, kit, passive) and all nine abilities (name, cooldown, ready attribute, colour). |
| `shared/features/classes/Classes` | Pure: which ability is in which slot, ownership, damage multipliers, "behind", execute. |
| `server/features/classes/ClassService` | The `ChooseClass` remote, the `Class` attribute, and `isPaused`. |

## The class is an attribute

`Class` on the Player: `""` until chosen, then a key of `Config.CLASSES`. The selection screen opens
and closes on it, the HUD and the ability key read it, and it survives respawns because it is on
the Player, not the character. `ClassService.choose` accepts a choice **once per session**: a
second one would hand a player in the middle of a fight full health and fresh cooldowns. Whether
the class may be changed mid-room is still open in the GDD.

Applying a class is `CombatService.applyClass`, passed to ClassService as a dependency: health and
max health, base walk speed (fed into `Defense.walkSpeed` like the old constant), cooldowns reset,
and weapons re-equipped. `onCharacter` reads the same attribute, so a respawn keeps the class.

## The pause

`ClassService.isPaused()` is true while any player in the server has no class. `EnemyService.step`,
`DropService.step`, and all player combat remotes in `CombatService` + `CombatInput` ignore input
while it is true — so nobody can attack frozen enemies, spend cooldowns, or spawn map drops while a
teammate is still on `ClassSelect`. With more than one player, one person on the selection screen
freezes the board for everybody; that is fine for testing and is the thing to revisit when the
lobby replaces the screen.

## Weapons

Each class names a `weapon` and an optional `offHandWeapon` (`Config.CLASSES`). They are `.rbxmx`
models generated by `scripts/generate-models.luau` (`WeaponSpikedMace`, `WeaponShield`,
`WeaponDagger`, `WeaponStaff`, plus legacy `WeaponGreatsword`) under the same convention as the test sword: `Grip` at the origin,
the weapon along -Z. Each model carries `GripPitch` / `GripYaw` / `GripRoll` attributes (degrees),
and `CombatService.equipWeapon` welds `Grip` to the hand's grip attachment turned by them — so how a
weapon is held lives with the weapon, not in a table in code. The staff is pitched 165° so it stands
upright with the orb above the head (measured: orb 3.2 studs above the hand).

`CombatAnim` provides role-specific guard and attack poses (`CombatAnim.blockPose(classId)` raises
the Tank's left-arm tower shield and keeps the Support's 165° staff vertical; `CombatAnim.evaluate`
uses forward staff casts for Support instead of horizontal sword slashes).

## Damage & Defense

Every player hit on an enemy goes through `CombatService.hitEnemy`: the base (with the damage
buff) times `Classes.outgoingMultiplier`, which is the class's `damageMultiplier` and, for the
Damage class, `+DPS_PASSIVE_BONUS` against a *vulnerable* enemy — one that is stunned
(`StunnedUntil`, e.g. from Tank War Cry or Support Shockwave), in its `recover` phase, or hit
from behind (`Classes.isBehind`).

Every hit on a player goes through `CombatService.hurt`, which multiplies incoming damage by:
- `Classes.incomingMultiplier` (Tank passive `-20%`),
- `Classes.bulwarkMultiplier` (Tank **Coraza** self-buff: **`-75%`** to all incoming damage while `BulwarkUntil` is active), and
- `Classes.protectMultiplier` (Support **Barrera** squad-wide buff: **`-45%`** to all living players while `ProtectBuffUntil` is active).

## Ranged Support Staff & Role-Specific Heavy Attacks

`Classes.isRanged(classId)` distinguishes the Support's **Báculo de energía** (`attackStyle = "ranged"`):
- **Basic combo (`Attack`):** Support does not melee-lunge (`CombatInput` uses `Config.STAFF_AIM_ASSIST_RANGE = 42` with zero step-in lunge) and `CombatService.attack` spawns server-authoritative traveling energy bolts (`playerBolts`, step 3 splashes in `Config.STAFF_BOLT_FINISHER_SPLASH`), rendered on all clients via `CombatVfx` `"staffBolt"` / `"boltImpact"`.
- **Heavy Attack (`HeavyAttack`, 2 s hold):**
  - **Tanque (`tank`):** *Impacto Sísmico* — circular seismic greatsword slam (`TANK_HEAVY_RADIUS`) that damages, shoves outward, and stuns (`EnemyService.stun`).
  - **Daño (`dps`):** *Desgarro* — twin rapid diagonal X-shaped dagger slashes (`DPS_HEAVY_SECOND_DELAY` apart) with neon echo.
  - **Apoyo (`support`):** *Orbe de Plasma* — long-range explosive arcane orb (`SUPPORT_HEAVY_RANGE`) that detonates on impact or max range for AoE damage and stun.

## Abilities

The three original abilities (`Torbellino`/`spin`, `Estocada`/`stinger`, `Corte Final`/`overdrive`)
keep their own remotes (`Spin`, `Stinger`, `OverDrive`) and their attribute names; each now also
refuses a player whose class does not own it. The six class ones share one contracted remote,
`UseAbility(id, aim)`, dispatched by `CombatService.useAbility` through `ABILITY_HANDLERS` after
the same checks (owned, not grabbed/blocking/stunned/charging, off cooldown). Every cooldown is
`Config.ABILITIES[id].readyAttribute` in server time, which is how the HUD draws any slot without
knowing which ability is in it.

| Ability | Class | What the server does |
|---|---|---|
| Rugido (`taunt`) | Tank | `EnemyService.stun` (`TAUNT_STUN_DURATION`, cancels windups/fuses/grabs) + `EnemyService.taunt` + light shock damage to every enemy in `TAUNT_RADIUS`. |
| Coraza (`bulwark`) | Tank | Sets `BulwarkUntil` (`BULWARK_DURATION`): self-buff cutting ALL incoming damage by `BULWARK_REDUCTION` (`75%`), shown as a HUD chip (`CORAZA -75%`) and ForceField aura. |
| Vórtice (`execute`) | Damage | Launches a server-stepped boomerang along `Classes.boomerangPosition` (`BOOMERANG_RANGE` out, homing back to the caster's live position), slicing enemies on both passes (with `Classes.executeDamage` bonus below `EXECUTE_THRESHOLD`). |
| Santuario (`healPulse`) | Support | Immediate `HEAL_ZONE_INITIAL_BURST` heal plus a persistent `HEAL_ZONE_DURATION` (`10 s`) ground zone (`healZones`) ticking `HEAL_ZONE_HEAL_PER_SECOND` (`14 HP/s`) to every player inside. |
| Barrera (`protectField`) | Support | Sets `ProtectBuffUntil` (`PROTECT_BUFF_DURATION`) on **every living player** in the squad (`-45%` incoming damage), shown as a HUD chip (`BARRERA +45%`) and ForceField aura. |
| Repulsión (`shockwave`) | Support | Damages, shoves (`EnemyService.shove`), slows, and **hard-stuns** (`EnemyService.stun`, `SHOCKWAVE_STUN_DURATION`) every enemy around the caster. |

The server `stepCombat` Heartbeat (started by `CombatService.startSupportTick`) drives `playerBolts`,
`boomerangs`, and `healZones`, while the 1-second tick runs the Support's passive regeneration aura.

Visuals for the new abilities come from a `CombatVfx` broadcast to every client, the caster
included, so nobody draws them twice; the caster's own pose (`Effects.classAbilityLocal`) is the
only local prediction. They are simple placeholders (rings, a dome, a shield plate, a slash), and
only the Execution has an animation (the combo finisher).

## Measured in Studio

Selection screen open with three cards and the board frozen (0 of 3 enemies moved in 2 s); a real
mouse click on "Elegir" gave `Class = tank`, 160 max health, greatsword and shield, the screen
closed and enemies moving. Damage class: 85 health, a dagger in each hand, HUD showing Stinger /
Ejecución / Over Drive; Execution killed a full-health Centinela. Support: 100 health, staff
upright; the shockwave pushed an enemy 10.6 studs (configured 10). Taunt and Muralla fired from the
keyboard and started their cooldowns; a Tank firing `Stinger` or `healPulse` by remote was refused.
